Timeline of Karachi

1729

Traditional date of the Kolachi fishing settlement.

1839

The British take control of Karachi.

1861

A railway line connects Karachi with the rest of the region.

1865

Frere Hall is completed.

1869

The Suez Canal opens and boosts Karachi's port.

1876

Muhammad Ali Jinnah is born in Karachi.

1884

The first trams begin running in the city.

1889

Empress Market is completed.

1892

Merewether Tower is built.

1927

Mohatta Palace is built.

1947

Karachi becomes the first capital of Pakistan.

1964

The Karachi Circular Railway opens.

1975

The tram service ends.

1999

The Circular Railway stops running.
