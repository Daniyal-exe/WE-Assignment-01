Old Cinemas

For many years, going to the cinema was one of the most popular outings in Karachi. Families, students and friends filled the big single-screen halls, especially around Saddar and the city center.

Some Well-Known Names

Regal Cinema

Located near Regal Chowk in Saddar. The area around it is still named after the cinema.

Capri Cinema

One of the most recognised cinemas on Shahrah-e-Liaquat, remembered for its large hall.

Rex Cinema

Another popular hall in the older part of the city that was loved by film fans.

What Happened?

Over time, many of these cinemas lost their audience. Home video, television and changing city life played a part. Some halls closed, and others were turned into shops or markets.
