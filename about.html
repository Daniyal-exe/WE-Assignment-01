About This Website

Forgotten Karachi is a small digital archive about the old buildings, neighborhoods and lost landmarks of Karachi. The goal is to share the city's hidden history in a simple and easy way.

Why This Project?

Karachi changes fast, and many old places are slowly disappearing. Writing them down and sharing them is one way to keep them from being forgotten.
